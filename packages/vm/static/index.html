How to Connect

WiFi / Hotspot Network Pairing

Follow these 4 simple steps to connect your mobile phone to your Windows PC.

1

Run Server on Windows PC

Open Terminal / PowerShell on your PC and launch the server:

npx vm2do

The console will activate and show your PC's IP Address and 4-digit Connect PIN.

2

Connect to Same Wi-Fi

Ensure both your mobile phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network (or phone Mobile Hotspot).

3

Scan QR Code or Enter IP

Option A (Easiest)

Open your phone camera and scan the terminal QR code. It will open the web app with IP & PIN pre-filled!

Option B (Manual)

On the Connect screen, type your PC's IP address (e.g. 10.15.235.86) and the 4-digit Connect PIN.

4

Start Controlling Desktop

Tap "Start & Connect". Once connected, use the trackpad gestures:

  • 1-Finger Drag: Smooth cursor movement
  • 1-Finger Tap: Left Click
  • 2-Finger Drag: Vertical Scrolling
  • Press & Hold Scroll Arrow: Continuous Auto-Scroll

Troubleshooting

Diagnostics & Fixes

Having trouble connecting or controlling your PC? Find quick solutions below.

Phone says "Connection Timed Out" or "Could not connect"

Common Causes: Windows Firewall blocking Node.js, or phone and PC are on different Wi-Fi networks.

Solutions:

  1. Check Windows Defender Firewall -> Allow an app -> Enable Private & Public access for Node.js.
  2. Verify phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi router network.
  3. If using a public/work Wi-Fi, turn on phone's Mobile Hotspot and connect PC to phone hotspot.
"Port 5000 is already in use by another process"

Cause: Another background server or app is using HTTP port 5000.

Solution: Start the server on a different open port using the --port argument:

node server.js --port=8080
Terminal QR Code won't scan on phone camera

Solutions:

  1. Open terminal link directly: click the Instant Mobile Link printed in the terminal console.
  2. View high-res QR image link printed in console: https://api.qrserver.com/...
  3. Or manually type the PC IP (e.g. 10.15.235.86) into the app Connect screen.
App disconnected after closing browser tab

Fixed in v2.5: App auto-restores connection when returning to the tab. If you accidentally clicked "Disconnect Device", enter your 4-digit PIN on the home screen and click "Start & Connect".
